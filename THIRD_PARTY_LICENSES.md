## Before the first public release

1. Inspect the build files, dependency manifests, lockfiles, vendored source, submodules, and downloaded dependencies. Identify exact versions or revisions.
2. Inspect all assets, including fonts, models, textures, audio, logos, and generated content. Verify provenance and redistribution permissions.
3. Record each third-party item's license, copyright notice, upstream URL, and applicable obligations in the inventory.
4. Preserve original license files and required notices; include source or offers for source where applicable.
5. Review compatibility with the intended Apache-2.0 distribution. If rights or compatibility cannot be confirmed, exclude the affected material from the public release until resolved.
6. Review the inventory whenever dependencies or assets change and before publishing binaries or asset packs.
